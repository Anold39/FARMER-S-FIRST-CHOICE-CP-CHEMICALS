How this connects to product matching: a Symptom option's "value" is the exact text the AI searches for among each product's diagnostic keywords (managed in Inventory Management's CSV upload). Adding a symptom option here only produces a real recommendation once at least one product's keywords actually contain that same text. Adding "root rot" here, for example, does nothing on its own — you'd also need to upload a CSV giving some product the keyword root rot. These two screens work together: this one controls what farmers can select; Inventory Management controls what it matches to.
