Bulk Resolve / Export via CSV

Export every ticket (including its response, if any) for offline review or record-keeping. To bulk-resolve, upload a CSV with header ticketId,agronomistName,response,recommendedProduct — useful if an agronomist reviewed several tickets offline and is uploading all the answers at once. Matches existing tickets by ticketId; unknown ticket IDs are skipped and listed below. recommendedProduct is optional and must exactly match a product name in the catalogue.
